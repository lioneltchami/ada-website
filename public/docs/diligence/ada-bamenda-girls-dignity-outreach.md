## 1. Terms of Reference

### Background

Menstrual hygiene remains a basic dignity issue for adolescent girls, especially where pads, underwear, soap, and safe storage items are expensive or inconsistent. This outreach is designed to respond to that need in Bamenda with a simple, practical, and respectful package that supports 25 girls with both supplies and information.

### Purpose

To provide 25 girls in Bamenda with menstrual dignity kits, hygiene items, and a short guided session on puberty, menstrual health, and self-care.

### Objectives

1. Distribute dignity kits to 25 girls.
2. Provide menstrual hygiene education in a safe, supportive setting.
3. Equip girls with basic items that help them manage monthly cycles with confidence.
4. Encourage open, age-appropriate conversations about body changes, hygiene, and dignity.
5. Document the outreach clearly for sponsor review and future reporting.

### Target Group

- 25 girls in Bamenda
- Priority should be given to girls from vulnerable households, schoolgirls with limited access to hygiene supplies, and girls who would benefit from a practical support package

### Proposed Activities

| Activity | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Registration and welcome | Simple attendance, consent, and distribution check-in |
| Dignity kit distribution | Pads or reusable pad kits, underwear, bucket/basin, soap, tissue, and other hygiene items |
| Health talk | Short puberty and menstrual health session led by a trusted female facilitator, nurse, teacher, or counselor |
| Q&A session | Private and respectful question period for the girls |
| Closing and encouragement | Photo documentation where permitted, closing remarks, and snacks |

### Expected Outcomes

- 25 girls receive dignity kits
- Girls leave with practical menstrual hygiene guidance
- Girls have items that improve comfort, hygiene, and confidence
- ADA has a clear attendance record, distribution record, and reporting trail

### Suggested Kit Contents Per Girl

- 2 to 3 packs of sanitary pads, or 1 reusable pad kit
- 2 pairs of underwear
- 1 small bucket or basin with lid
- 1 bar of soap
- 1 detergent sachet or small pack
- 1 tissue pack
- 1 simple menstrual hygiene information sheet

### Budget Notes

- If reusable pad kits are available at good quality and fair price, ADA can shift part of the pad budget into reusable options.

## 4. Monitoring and Safeguarding

- Use a female facilitator where possible.
- Keep the session respectful, age-appropriate, and private.
- Avoid exposing personal details in public photos or captions.
- Collect attendance and distribution records.
- Keep receipts for all purchases.
- If a girl requests follow-up support, note it discreetly and follow ADA safeguarding practice.
