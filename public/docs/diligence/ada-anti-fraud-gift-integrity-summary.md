# ADA Anti-Fraud and Gift Integrity Summary

**Organization:** Apoti Development Association (ADA)  
**Registration:** N° 415/G.37/D14/VolI/SAAJP  
**Last updated:** May 2026

## Purpose

This summary explains how ADA protects donor funds, avoids conflicts of interest, and handles restricted gifts responsibly.

## Core commitments

ADA prohibits:

- Bribery or improper payments
- Misappropriation of funds
- Falsification of records
- Undisclosed conflicts of interest in procurement or program delivery
- Use of restricted gifts outside the confirmed purpose

## Gift review

ADA may decline, pause, return, or request additional information about a gift if the source, purpose, restrictions, sponsor identity, or payment path cannot be responsibly verified.

## Conflict of interest

Team members, volunteers, and board members should disclose personal, family, business, or financial interests that could affect procurement, beneficiary selection, or program decisions.

## Documentation

Project expenses should be supported by budgets, receipts, invoices, signed attestations, distribution lists, field notes, or reports where appropriate.

## Reporting concerns

Concerns may be reported to info@apotidev.org with the subject line "Gift Integrity" or "Anti-Fraud Concern."
