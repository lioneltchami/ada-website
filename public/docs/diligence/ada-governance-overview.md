## Oversight practices

ADA's public governance approach includes:

- Board oversight of organizational direction and annual budgets
- Defined program roles for project planning and delivery
- Local coordination through community leaders, schools, clinics, churches, and partner sites where appropriate
- Public annual reports and project records
- Sponsor reporting matched to sponsorship size and agreement

## Conflict-of-interest and gift integrity

Board, team, and volunteer participants are expected to disclose personal, family, business, or financial interests that could affect procurement, beneficiary selection, or program decisions.

Restricted gifts are reviewed before payment. Program leadership confirms purpose, feasibility, reporting expectations, and recognition terms. Board-level oversight is used for larger, unusual, or higher-risk restrictions before funds are committed.

ADA may decline, pause, return, or request more information about a gift if the source, purpose, restrictions, sponsor identity, or payment path cannot be responsibly verified.

## Sponsor review path

Sponsors may request additional governance information, project references, or leadership calls through the sponsor inquiry form.
