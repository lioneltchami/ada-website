Fund measurable care for widows, orphans, and young women in Cameroon.

Apoti Development Association (ADA) is a Cameroon-registered nonprofit association serving vulnerable families through education support, widow relief, skills training, food assistance, health referrals, clean water, and field follow-up.

What Funds Buy

  • $25/month helps school supplies and child support.
  • $2,500-$5,000 can sponsor a bounded project.
  • $10,000+ can support a measurable program cycle.

Tax and Payment Note

ADA is registered in Cameroon. International gifts are not represented as tax-deductible unless a qualified fiscal sponsor or registered charity partner is confirmed in writing and published with receipt limitations. Online gifts use Stripe card payments in USD; eligible wallets may appear when supported. Invoice, bank, wire, restricted-gift, or sponsorship-agreement requests are reviewed before payment instructions are shared.
