Updated: May 2026

This scorecard gives sponsors a lean view of ADA's public impact indicators. It is designed for sponsor review and internal approval files. Metrics are marked as measured, field records, or estimated so readers can understand the strength of each indicator.

## Measurement Notes

- Measured: a count or financial figure reported from project or annual records.
- Field records: a figure supported by local field documentation such as attendance lists, distribution notes, photos, receipts, or project close-out records.
- Estimated: a planning or allocation figure that should not be read as independently audited.

## What This Scorecard Does Not Claim

- It does not claim audited financial statements.
- It does not claim independent third-party verification.
- It does not claim tax-deductible giving status outside Cameroon.
- It does not claim outcomes that ADA has not measured, such as long-term disease reduction or permanent household income change.
