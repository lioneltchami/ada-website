# ADA Sponsor Readiness Packet

Apoti Development Association (ADA) is a Cameroon-registered nonprofit association serving widows, orphans, young women, internally displaced families, students, and vulnerable households through practical community programs.

## Sponsor Options

| Level | Typical amount | Best fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community Sponsor | $500-$1,000 | Supplies, food support, seasonal giving, urgent household essentials |
| Project Sponsor | $2,500-$5,000 | A defined project with budget, people served, outcome summary, and final report |
| Program Sponsor | $10,000+ | A full program season, recurring support cycle, or multi-family response |
| Strategic Partner | Custom | Larger campaign cycle, capacity support, recurring support, or custom community initiative |

Online card gifts currently process in USD through Stripe. Sponsors who need another currency, invoice, bank transfer, or a larger restricted gift should contact ADA before payment so the right path can be confirmed in writing.

## What Sponsors Receive

- Receipt, invoice, or sponsorship confirmation for records
- Clear use-of-funds summary
- Project update or campaign recap
- Recognition by name or logo when approved by the sponsor
- Public recognition, internal acknowledgment, or full anonymity by preference
- Photos or stories when appropriate and consented
- Quarterly impact email for program-level sponsorships
- Final report for project and program sponsorships
- Optional social media thank-you when appropriate
- Employee, church, school, or diaspora group presentation when useful
- Optional leadership call for larger sponsorships

## Due Diligence Materials

ADA can provide or point sponsors to:

- Cameroon registration certificate

- Financial management policy
- Project budgets or campaign summaries where applicable

Public documents are available at:

- Resource library: https://apotidev.org/resources

- Project archive: https://apotidev.org/projects/archive

## Use of Funds

ADA's current public allocation model is:

- 80% program delivery
- 15% program operations
- 5% administration

Program spending is documented through budgets, receipts, attendance lists, photos, field notes, or project reports where appropriate.

## Reporting Cadence

Reporting is matched to the sponsor level and agreement:

- Community Sponsor: short update or annual donor communication
- Project Sponsor: project update, outcome summary, and final report
- Program Sponsor: quarterly impact email and final report
- Strategic Partner: agreed cadence, usually including a written summary and optional briefing call

Restricted gifts or major sponsorships should be discussed before payment so ADA can confirm the funding purpose, reporting expectations, and any recognition preferences.

## Sponsor Payment Options

- Stripe card giving is available online for one-time or monthly USD donations.
- Invoice, sponsorship agreement, or purchase-order style requests can start through the sponsor inquiry form.
- Bank, wire, or direct transfer details can be arranged by request before payment where available.
- Corporate matching is welcome, but employers should confirm eligibility because ADA is Cameroon-registered and not a Canadian charity or US 501(c)(3).
- Restricted gifts should be discussed before payment so ADA can confirm purpose, reporting, and recognition expectations.
- Check gifts should only be used if a qualified fiscal sponsor or registered charity partner is confirmed in writing.

## Legal And Tax Note

ADA is registered in Cameroon. At this time, contributions are not represented as tax-deductible in Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, or the European Union unless a qualified registered partner is confirmed in writing. ADA can issue a receipt, invoice, or sponsorship confirmation for personal or organizational records, but does not provide tax advice.

Corporate sponsorships may be treated differently from charitable donations depending on country, business structure, and benefits received. Sponsors should consult their own tax or legal advisor.
