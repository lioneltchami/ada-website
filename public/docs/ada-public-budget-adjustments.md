Projects marked `reported / reconstructed; see basis` retain their detailed category schedules and evidence qualifications in the project reports; they do not establish verified spending or a current account balance. Projects marked `reported; document review pending` contain qualified summaries while revised documents are reviewed; proposed item prices are excluded. Projects marked `reported; project documents available` link to source-supported project accounts; they are not audited. Projects marked `itemized budget published; not actual expenditure` show the planning budget only, without an actual expense or a public spending target. Projects marked `under review` have no numeric public target in this worksheet. Projects marked `adjusted` should be reviewed with receipts, bank/mobile money history, or staff confirmation before publication as a final spend record.
