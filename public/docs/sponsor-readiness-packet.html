  • Project update or campaign recap

  • Public recognition, internal acknowledgment, or full anonymity by preference

  • Final report for project and program sponsorships

  • Optional leadership call for larger sponsorships

Use of Funds

ADA’s current public allocation model is:

  • 80% program delivery
  • 15% program operations
  • 5% administration

Program spending is documented through budgets, receipts, attendance lists, photos, field notes, or project reports where appropriate.

Reporting Cadence

Reporting is matched to the sponsor level and agreement:

  • Community Sponsor: short update or annual donor communication
  • Project Sponsor: project update, outcome summary, and final report
  • Program Sponsor: quarterly impact email and final report
  • Strategic Partner: agreed cadence, usually including a written summary and optional briefing call

Restricted gifts or major sponsorships should be discussed before payment so ADA can confirm the funding purpose, reporting expectations, and any recognition preferences.

Sponsor Payment Options

  • Stripe card giving is available online for one-time or monthly USD donations; eligible wallet options may appear when supported by Stripe, the donor’s device, and browser settings.
  • Invoice, sponsorship agreement, or purchase-order style requests should start through the sponsor inquiry form.
  • Bank, wire, or direct-transfer requests are reviewed manually before ADA shares any payment instructions.
  • PayPal, Venmo, and ACH should not be treated as active ADA payment paths until operational setup and reconciliation are confirmed.
  • Corporate matching is welcome, but employers should confirm eligibility because ADA is Cameroon-registered and not a Canadian charity or US 501(c)(3).
  • Restricted gifts should be discussed before payment so ADA can confirm purpose, reporting, and recognition expectations.
  • Check gifts should only be used if a qualified fiscal sponsor or registered charity partner is confirmed in writing.

Legal And Tax Note

ADA is registered in Cameroon. At this time, contributions are not represented as tax-deductible in Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, or the European Union unless a qualified registered partner is confirmed in writing. ADA can issue a receipt, invoice, or sponsorship confirmation for personal or organizational records, but does not provide tax advice.

Corporate sponsorships may be treated differently from charitable donations depending on country, business structure, and benefits received. Sponsors should consult their own tax or legal advisor.

If ADA confirms a qualified fiscal sponsor or registered charity partner in Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, or the European Union, ADA will publish the partner name, country, donation instructions, eligible tax treatment, and receipt limitations before promoting that pathway.
