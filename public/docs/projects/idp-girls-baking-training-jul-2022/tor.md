# Practical baking skills for displaced girls

Apoti Development Association
Sharing Christ's Love, Restoring Hope
Practical baking skills for displaced girls
Retrospective terms of reference | July 2022 | Molyko, Buea
Why this project mattered
Displacement can interrupt education, family support and opportunities to earn a living. We
wanted to give young women a practical introduction to making food they could prepare and
sell, together with encouragement and modest starting resources. Our Christian commitment to
care was expressed through our team’s time, the trainer’s teaching and the funds entrusted to
this outreach. A short workshop could open a door to practice; it could not guarantee a
livelihood.
Who, where and how we selected participants
Through community enquiries and word of mouth, our team identified internally displaced
teenage girls to invite. Our account records 15 participants. We arranged to use the Sunday
school hall at Full Gospel Mission, Molyko, Buea, with the pastor’s permission. The precise day
in July has not been established.
Scope and delivery approach
The intended learning opportunity was practical baking and frying: cakes, fish pie, meat pie and
puff-puff. Auntie Dorothy was invited to lead the session after we agreed the arrangements with
her. Our team organised ingredients, equipment and the cooking area. The approximately
four-hour format combined prayer and welcome, encouragement, demonstrations and
supervised practice by some participants.
Starting resources and responsibilities
Our team arranged transparent buckets for carrying and selling products, participant support of
5,000 FCFA each, and distribution of remaining flour and oil. Dorothy’s role was to demonstrate
preparation and guide practice; our team handled invitations, venue arrangements, purchasing
and distribution. Assistance was available regardless of participation in prayer or worship. No
identifying participant information is included in this document.
Resources and intended follow-up
Funding of 400,000 FCFA was sent. The original written budget totals 342,750 FCFA, including
75,000 in participant support. The companion report preserves the original plan separately from
actual spending. Its arithmetic conflicts remain unresolved; reconciliation questions are kept in
our private working papers. Follow-up could record participants’ voluntary accounts of practice,
without claiming measured income or business success.
