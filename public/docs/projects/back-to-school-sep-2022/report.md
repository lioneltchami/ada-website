Apoti Development Association
Sharing Christ’s Love, Restoring Hope
Back to School in Buea
Project completion report
12 August 2022 • Buea, Cameroon • 40 students reached
Our team visited the school we remember as Buea Regional Secondary School to provide
learning materials and encourage students as they prepared for the school year.
Exercise books, writing materials and school bags from our August project photographs.
A visit organized with the school
With permission arranged in advance, the
teachers brought the students together in a
classroom. They helped us share the
materials. Working with the school allowed
us to meet the children in a familiar place and
draw on the teachers’ knowledge of their
needs.
What we provided
We shared books and exercise books, school
bags, pens and other school materials. Our
account records 40 students reached. The
children did not all receive the same brands
of bags, exercise books or pens. This was not a
documented distribution of 40 identical kits.
Item quantities and individual values remain
unestablished; the financial report and
detailed schedule preserve that distinction.

Encouragement and care
Back to School in Buea • 12 August 2022
School bags in different styles. The photographs
do not establish identical packages or equal

Encouragement rooted in our faith
The gathering included welcome and
encouragement, singing and worship, Bible
reading and prayer alongside the material
support. We encouraged the children to take
their studies seriously, be good students and do
their best. Our Christian faith is part of why we
give our time and resources to this work.
Assistance was available whether or not a student joined the prayer or worship. Practical
support and encouragement were offered together, without making religious participation a
condition of receiving help.
The response we remember
Families later asked the school how to contact us and got in touch to thank us. Their response
mattered to our team: the materials and the care behind the visit had been noticed beyond the
classroom.
We can report that school materials were shared and 40 students were reached. We did not
measure changes in grades, school attendance or longer-term learning outcomes. We therefore
do not claim that the visit produced those results.
Financial accountability and follow-up
Our account records 220,000 FCFA sent and 205,000 FCFA of reported spending, including
transport, withdrawal fees and the operating costs in our confirmed account. The balance
remaining in our accounts after this project was 15,000 FCFA, held in mobile money for later use.
The financial report and detailed schedule explain these figures; later account movements are
separate.
Our team normally meets after projects to discuss what went well and what could improve. If
further records or corrected details become available, we will update this account.
