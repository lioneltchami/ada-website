# Back to school in Buea

## Terms of reference

The PDF is the authoritative layout, including tables and photographs. This text companion preserves its evidence qualifications.

Apoti Development Association
Sharing Christ’s Love, Restoring Hope
Back to School in Buea
Retrospective terms of reference
Project date: Friday, 12 August 2022 • Place: Buea, South-West Region, Cameroon • School: The
school we remember as Buea Regional Secondary School
Why we chose this project
We wanted children to begin the school year with useful learning materials and
encouragement. Our team asked around in the community and visited schools to understand
where support was most needed. We noticed children without adequate school bags, books or
clothing. From our conversations and observations, the needs at this school appeared greater
than at others nearby.
We chose the school deliberately. People gave their time, money and effort, and we wanted
that support to reach children in need. Providing books, pens and bags was one way to share
the love of Jesus and support their everyday learning.
Who the project was intended to support
The project was intended to support students facing a shortage of essential learning
materials, selected with the school’s help. Its scope was school materials and encouragement,
including books and exercise books, school bags, pens and other school materials. Clothing
was an observed need, not a recalled distributed item. The report records 40 students
reached.
How we prepared and who was involved
Our team visited the principal in advance, explained why we wanted to come, obtained
permission and signed the school register for the planned visit. Teachers gathered the
students in a classroom and helped with distribution.
Our usual approach is to let the school identify children who need support across classes,
because teachers know their students. Our team handles coordination, purchasing and
delivery; school staff help organize and distribute.
Our approach to the gathering
We welcome the students and teachers, offer Christian encouragement and share materials
with the school's help. Our usual programme allows about three hours for the opening,
worship, Bible reading, prayer and distribution. This describes our usual format, not a timed
record of this particular visit. Assistance is available regardless of participation in prayer or
worship.
Resources and reporting basis
The report details 220,000 FCFA sent, 205,000 FCFA reported spending and a balance remaining
in our accounts of 15,000 FCFA after the project.
