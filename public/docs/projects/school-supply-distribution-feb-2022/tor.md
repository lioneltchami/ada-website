Apoti Development Association

School supplies and encouragement
Retrospective terms of reference
Wednesday, 16 February 2022 | Lycée de Buea | Buea, Cameroon
Why we chose this project
We chose Lycée de Buea after hearing that its existing prayer or Bible club needed
encouragement. We understood that its activities had slowed and wanted to offer Christian
encouragement and practical support. Our purpose was not simply to visit: we wanted our team's
time and resources to offer something useful to the children, both through the materials we
brought and through the care we showed.
The intended activity combined encouragement with a donation of school supplies and other
useful items. It was a separate school visit, not the August back-to-school project.
Who and what the visit was for
The visit was for students at Lycée de Buea. The intended materials were school bags, exercise
books, pens, mathematical sets, buckets and utensils. We do not specify a target headcount or
equal packages because those details are not established in this account.
Preparation, delivery and responsibilities
Our team visited the school beforehand and obtained permission. The approach was to meet the
students in a classroom, pray, read the Bible, encourage them and distribute the materials. Our
team arranged the visit and brought the supplies; the school's permission made the classroom
visit possible. No individual staff assignments are attributed without a supporting record.
Sharing the love of Jesus is part of our purpose. Assistance was available regardless of whether a
student participated in prayer or worship. Children's names and identifying photographs are
excluded from this document set.
Scope and resources
The planned February cleanup did not take place. The donation and encouragement visit went
ahead.
We sent 215,000 FCFA for the visit. Withdrawal was recalled at about 5,000 FCFA and transport at
10,000-15,000 FCFA, with the balance used for supplies. The companion report sets out the
funding and recalled operating costs. Exact purchase prices and the closing balance are not
established.
