Project completion report and financial account
Why this visit mattered
We heard that the school's prayer or Bible
club, which had existed before, needed
encouragement. We chose to respond with
our presence, Christian encouragement and
practical gifts for the students. The visit
brought together the time our team gave and
the money entrusted to the project, so that
our care was expressed in both words and
useful materials.
What our team did
We visited the school in advance and
obtained permission. On Wednesday, 16
February 2022, we met students in a
classroom, prayed with them, read the Bible
and encouraged them. We then gave the
students the materials we had brought.

Useful materials for students
The supplies included school bags, exercise books, pens and mathematical sets, together with
buckets and utensils. These were for the students. Quantities and recipient-by-recipient
allocations are not recorded in the information available to us, so we do not describe identical
kits or calculate a cost per child.
What was delivered and what changed
We can report the school visit, encouragement and distribution of the six material categories. A
verified student count, improved school results and the revival of the club are not established in
the information available to us.
The February cleanup that had been discussed did not take place. This did not cancel the
donation visit.
Faith, care and accountability
Prayer, Bible reading and encouragement reflected our Christian purpose. Assistance was
available whether or not a student joined in prayer or worship. We have kept children's names
and identifying photographs out of the report.
Our general practice is to meet as a team after a project to discuss what went well, timing and
improvements. We do not present that practice as a documented February debrief. The following
financial page distinguishes funds reported as sent from recalled operating costs and amounts
not established.
Reporting basis: Prepared some time after the project was carried out, using our team's account
and available working files. This retrospective account is not an independently audited
statement. The financial page explains which amounts are recalled or established.

Understanding the balance
The transfer less recalled charges does not establish the actual purchase total or a closing
balance. If transport was at the lower end of the recalled range, the difference cannot
automatically be assigned to extra supplies or treated as a balance remaining in our accounts. No
figure in this report is today's mobile-money balance.
Supporting records
