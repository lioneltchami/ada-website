Apoti Development Association
Sharing Christ’s Love, Restoring Hope
School visit in Buea
The heart of the visit
We wanted our time and resources to offer students more than a brief delivery of supplies. A
school visit creates space to encourage young people while meeting practical needs. Our
Christian identity shapes that commitment to care, and receiving support does not depend on
joining prayer or worship.
The activity recorded
The earlier May project account describes a school visit with mentorship and encouragement,
food and refreshments, and educational materials or handouts.
Students, school and period
What the support covered
The earlier account lists food and refreshments, educational materials and the operating costs
needed to organise the visit. Transport, communication and withdrawal-related costs are
included in the detailed reconstructed allocation. No exact item quantities, unit prices, identical
packages or specific stationery types are inferred from the category totals.
Delivery and outcome
The documented scope is a school visit involving practical support and encouragement. The
available account does not establish lasting academic progress, guaranteed attendance, a new
formal partnership or a measurable effect on students. This visit followed our separate February
school outreach.
Our responsibility to students
We want the account of our visit to respect the students and school community. The financial
account describes the support allocated to this visit without attributing an equal package or an
equal cost to each student.
Prepared some time after the project was carried out. This account brings together the earlier school-visit
documents and subsequent clarifications. The financial pages distinguish the original budget, the approved
reconstructed allocation and unresolved cash accounting.
