School visit in Buea
Why we made this visit
Our school outreach is a practical expression of our Christian commitment to care for children
and young people. Alongside material support, we value time spent listening, encouraging
students and recognising the effort involved in their education. The May school visit brought this
purpose into a school setting in Buea.
Who and where
Purpose and scope
The earlier project account describes a school visit with encouragement and mentorship, food
and refreshments, and educational materials or handouts. Its intended purpose was to support
students through both practical provision and personal encouragement. This document does not
establish a measured learning outcome or an ongoing formal school partnership.
Organisation and responsibilities
Our usual school-outreach approach involves community inquiries, contact with school
leadership and coordination with teachers before a visit. Our team organises supplies and
transport, engages with students and works with school staff during delivery. This is our
established approach, not a verified minute-by-minute record of the May visit.
Resources
The earlier written budget was 180,000 FCFA. The reconstructed allocation comprises 165,000
FCFA in cash-cost categories and a separate 15,000 FCFA in-kind refreshments value. This is a
reconstructed allocation, not proof that 180,000 FCFA was transferred in cash. The companion
report contains every category and its financial basis.
