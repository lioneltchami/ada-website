## Owner-recalled funding flow

The owner recalls that about 220,000 CFA francs was sent for the outreach. About 5,000 CFA francs was used for withdrawal costs and about 10,000 CFA francs for transportation, leaving an estimated 205,000 CFA francs for supplies. These are approximate recollections and have not been checked against receipts, account statements, or a cashbook.

The owner recalls the supply categories as **buckets, African fabric wrappers, soap, oil, foodstuffs, and bags**. No amount is assigned to an individual category. The available information does not establish quantities, unit prices, exact expenditure, or a remaining cash balance.

No cash-assistance amount is included in this review note because the owner has not confirmed that cash was distributed as part of this project. This is not a finding that no cash assistance occurred.
