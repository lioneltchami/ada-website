Widows' home outreach
Retrospective terms of reference
Saturday, 12 March 2022 | Muea, Buea
Why we chose this outreach
Our Christian commitment to care for our neighbours calls us to notice needs that can remain
hidden behind a front door. Through conversations in the community and with churches, our
team sought out widows facing particular hardship, including women caring for children and
women whose health or mobility made daily life more difficult. We wanted to respond with
useful household supplies and personal encouragement, rather than choose recipients or
purchases at random.
Who and what the project was for
The outreach was intended for widows identified through those local enquiries and
conversations. Our approach was to listen to each woman, understand the support she needed
and arrange a home visit with her permission. Support took the form of goods, not cash grants.
The completion report records an approximate reach of 20 widows; this is not an
attendance-register count.
Preparation and responsibilities
Our leaders gathered information and brought it back to the team for discussion and agreement
on the visit date. Conversations with the women informed the shopping list. Our team arranged
purchases, transport and the sequence of home visits. Supplies were to be kept at a nearby
staging point and carried to each home in turn, rather than asking women with limited mobility
to attend a central distribution.
Delivery approach and care
At each home we would greet our host, offer encouragement, read the Bible and pray, then leave
the supplies intended for her. Our faith motivates this service; assistance was available
regardless of participation in prayer or worship. Respect for privacy and dignity meant keeping
personal health details and recipient names out of the partner report.
Our recollection is that about 220,000 FCFA was sent, with withdrawal recalled at 5,000 FCFA and
transport at about 10,000 FCFA. These figures give an approximate goods amount of 205,000
FCFA. The companion report separates recalled totals from reconstructed category allocations;
individual purchase prices have not been verified. Our usual closing practice is a team
discussion of delivery, timing and improvements.
