## Summary

The owner identifies this project as a Women's Day widow outreach in March 2022. The owner recalls that the supply list included buckets, African fabric wrappers, soap, oil, foodstuffs, and bags.

The owner recalls about **220,000 CFA francs sent**, about **5,000 CFA francs** in withdrawal costs, and about **10,000 CFA francs** for transportation. On that recollection, approximately **205,000 CFA francs** remained for supplies. These figures are estimates, not a reconciled account of actual expenditure.
